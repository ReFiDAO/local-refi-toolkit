## 🌟 TLDR

DeCleanup is a Web3 platform that tokenizes environmental cleanup efforts, allowing communities to earn $DCU tokens and dynamic Impact Products for verified cleanup activities. Users submit geotagged before/after photos of cleanups, which are verified by the team (later transitioning to community verification). This creates sustainable incentives for environmental action while building on-chain proof of real-world impact through gamification, referral systems, and future DeFi integration with staking opportunities.

---

## 👋 Introduction

**Playbook Intro:** This playbook demonstrates how to implement DeCleanup dApp use - a decentralized application that bridges Web3 incentives with real-world environmental action. It solves the problem of lacking incentives for environmental volunteering by creating a tokenized reward system for verified cleanup activities. The platform transforms individual and community cleanup efforts into measurable, onchain environmental commodities.

**Target Audience:** This playbook is designed for:

- Local environmental groups and NGOs seeking for additional support
- ReFi communities wanting to create measurable ecological impact
- Community organizers looking to gamify sustainability initiatives
- Web3-native users interested in regenerative finance
- Requires basic smartphone usage and crypto wallet setup (MetaMask)

---

## 📚 Playbook Description

**Overview:** DeCleanup creates a decentralized ecosystem where environmental cleanup activities are verified, tokenized, and rewarded. Users submit proof of impact through geotagged photos, earn dynamic NFTs called "Impact Products" that evolve with their contributions, and accumulate $DCU tokens. The platform progresses from team-based verification to community-driven validation through staking mechanisms.

**Impact:** The playbook creates ecological impact by incentivizing consistent cleanup activities, social impact through community building and gamification, and economic impact by introducing Web3 rewards that remain within the ecosystem rather than being immediately off-ramped to traditional finance.

**Innovation Highlight:** DeCleanup uniquely combines:

- Dynamic Impact Products that evolve based on user contributions
- Proof of Impact verification using geotagged photography
- Multi-phase progression from centralized to decentralized verification
- Integration with broader ReFi ecosystem
- Future cross-chain compatibility and DeFi staking opportunities

**Local Context:** The platform adapts to different environments through:

- Flexible cleanup locations (beaches, parks, streets, forests)
- Multi-chain deployment for regional blockchain preferences
- Future feature of Impact Circles allowing local organizers to create collaborative campaigns
- Future feature of optional stablecoin reward pools (e.g., $USDGLO)
- Partnerships with regional environmental organizations

## ⚙️ How It Works

![DeCleanup Network Diagram](https://prod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/98673c04-e3cf-4b0a-8f3c-a85c8332f21e/c17c74d8-6498-44ce-b8f9-fa2fa9fd2571/Decleanup_network.png)

### Implementation Process

### Step 1: Community Setup & Wallet Preparation

1. **Install MetaMask Wallet.** (recommended)
   - Download MetaMask browser extension or mobile app
   - Create a new wallet or import existing one
   - Secure your seed phrase safely
   - Understand basic wallet operations

2. **Access DeCleanup dApp**
   - Visit app.decleanup.net
   - Connect your MetaMask wallet
   - Complete initial profile setup

3. **Understand the Dashboard**
   - View your Impact Product NFT
   - Check $DCU token balance
   - See cleanup history and streaks
   - Explore leaderboards and community

### Step 2: Conducting Your First Cleanup

1. **Prepare for Cleanup**
   - Choose a location needing environmental cleanup
   - Gather supplies (bags, gloves, tools)
   - Ensure phone has GPS enabled
   - Review photo submission guidelines

2. **Document the Cleanup**
   - Take clear "BEFORE" photo of polluted area
   - Ensure GPS location is captured
   - Conduct the cleanup activity
   - Take clear "AFTER" photo showing results
   - Include reference objects for scale

3. **Submit Proof of Impact**
   - Upload before/after photos to dApp
   - Add description and location details
   - Submit for verification
   - Await team review and approval

### Step 3: Claiming Rewards & Building Streaks

1. **Wait for Verification**
   - Team reviews submissions for authenticity
   - Typical review time: 24-48 hours
   - Receive notification when approved

2. **Claim Your Impact Product**
   - Dynamic NFT evolves with your contributions
   - Visual representation of your environmental impact
   - Stored in your wallet, viewable on OpenSea

3. **Build Streaks & Earn Bonuses**
   - Consecutive cleanups earn streak bonuses
   - Weekly and monthly challenges available
   - Referral rewards for bringing friends

### Step 4: Community Engagement & Growth

1. **Track Your Progress**
   - Monitor cleanup count and impact metrics
   - View your position on leaderboards
   - Share achievements on social media

2. **Organize Group Cleanups**
   - Create local cleanup events
   - Invite community members
   - Coordinate through Telegram groups
   - Amplify impact through collective action

3. **Prepare for Future Features**
   - Impact Circles (community campaigns)
   - Community-based verification (stake $DCU to validate)
   - Stablecoin reward pools
   - Cross-chain deployment

### Other Tips & Best Practices

**Success Factors:**
- Ensure clear photo submission guidelines
- Maintain consistent verification standards
- Build strong community engagement through social features
- Partner with established environmental organizations
- Create educational content about Web3 onboarding

**Common Challenges:**
- Photo quality and verification disputes - Solution: Clear guidelines and feedback loops
- User onboarding to Web3 - Solution: Simplified wallet setup tutorials
- Maintaining verification quality at scale - Solution: Gradual transition to community validation
- Preventing fraudulent submissions - Solution: Multi-layered verification with geotag requirements

## 📌 Case Studies

**Current Implementation Status:** DeCleanup is currently in Version 2.1 with active development toward Version 2.2. The platform has established partnerships within the ReFi ecosystem, including [Regen Bazaar](https://regenbazaar.com/), [Atlantis](https://www.atlantisp2p.com/), [Detrash Global](https://www.detrashtoken.com/en) and has different strategies on how to integrate with their ecosystems.

**Success Stories:**

![DeCleanup Success Metrics](https://prod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/98673c04-e3cf-4b0a-8f3c-a85c8332f21e/c9d4b423-32a6-4a8a-a044-3698e1e12aa2/Decleanup_network_%282%29.png)

**Lessons Learned:**
- **Verification Quality**: Manual team verification in Phase 1 ensures high standards before decentralizing
- **User Experience**: Simple photo submission process is crucial for non-Web3 native users
- **Ecosystem Integration**: Building within existing ReFi infrastructure accelerates adoption
- **Incentive Design**: Streak bonuses and referral systems significantly boost engagement
- **Technical Architecture**: Planning for multi-chain deployment from the start prevents future migration issues

**Key Implementation Insights:**
- Start with centralized verification to establish quality standards
- Focus on clear user onboarding for Web3 newcomers
- Build partnerships within the existing ReFi ecosystem early
- Design tokenomics to prevent speculation while rewarding real impact
- Plan technical architecture for future decentralization from launch
