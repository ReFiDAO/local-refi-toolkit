*An essential guide to launching your first ReFi community event and setting the stage for a thriving local ReFi Node.*

---

## 🌟 TL;DR

Hosting an 'Intro to ReFi' Meetup is a great way to gather local regenerative leaders, entrepreneurs, technologists, builders, and community builders to foster deep connections and collaborations. Follow this guide to design, promote, and execute a high-impact event that builds momentum for your Local ReFi Node.

---

## 👋 Introduction

### What is this playbook about?

This playbook provides a step-by-step guide to organizing and hosting a **high-energy, engaging** introductory meetup on **Regenerative Finance (ReFi)**.

### Why host an 'Intro to ReFi' Meetup?

- 🌍 **Grow your local ReFi community**
- 🤝 **Foster deep human relationships** among regenerative leaders
- 💡 **Educate and inspire** newcomers about the power of ReFi
- 🚀 **Create opportunities** for future projects, collaborations, and funding

### Target Audience

This event is ideal for:

- **Community builders** looking to launch a **ReFi Local Node**
- **Regenerative leaders** from public, private, and nonprofit sectors
- **Web3 builders, entrepreneurs, and technologists**
- **Anyone passionate about finance, regeneration, and positive impact**

---

## 📚 Playbook Description

### Overview

The first event sets the **tone for your community** and helps build momentum by gathering frequently and attracting **top talent and value-aligned capital**.

### Key Design Considerations

✅ **Invite regenerative leaders** from across sectors (public, private, nonprofit)

✅ **Choose a strategic location** to attract people from the local community

✅ **Prioritize human connection**—design the event to foster meaningful interactions

✅ **Deliver short, high-energy, compelling content**

✅ **Host a community dinner afterward** for deeper conversations

✅ **Capture content (photos/videos)** to share online & inspire more participation

✅ **Ask for feedback** and improve the experience over time

✅ **Manage your budget wisely**—seek sponsorships, discounts, or venue support

### Innovation Highlight

By combining **Liberating Structures** and **Open Space Technology**, you can encourage **interactive, decentralized, and engaging discussions** rather than passive listening.

💡 [Liberating Structures Overview](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/)

💡 [Open Space Technology Explained](https://openspaceworld.org/wp2/what-is/)

## 💰 Business Model & Budgeting

### Funding & Sponsorships

To cover event costs and create sustainable funding for ongoing meetups, consider:

#### **Web3 Fundraising Platforms**
- Apply to funding rounds (Gitcoin Grants, Regen Coordination, Celo Public Goods)
- Create crowdfunding campaigns for series of meetups

#### **Sponsorships from ReFi & Web3 Projects**
- Reach out to protocols and DAOs aligned with your mission
- Offer sponsor visibility at the event and in marketing materials

#### **Venue & Service Partnerships**
- Negotiate discounts with local venues
- Partner with cafes, coworking spaces, or community centers
- Seek in-kind donations (food, drinks, AV equipment)

💡 **Tip:** Many Web3 funding rounds (like **Gitcoin Grants, Regen Coordination, or Celo Public Goods**) have open calls for proposals—apply to **secure funding for multiple meetups** rather than one-off events.

💡 **Tip:** Manage your budgets wisely—ask for support from the **venue, vendors, and community members.**

## ⚙️ How It Works

### Step-by-Step Implementation

### 1️⃣ Announce Your Event (Even Without Full Details!)

- Set a **date** and **publicly announce** the event
- Create a **landing page** (e.g., [lu.ma](http://lu.ma/)) for RSVPs
- Share on **social media & community channels**

### 2️⃣ Secure Venue & Logistics

- Book a **local, accessible space**
- Arrange **seating, sound, and visuals** for engagement
- Plan for **food & drinks** (community dinner recommended!)

### 3️⃣ Design an Engaging Agenda

- **Intro to ReFi Presentation** *(ReFi DAO provides a detailed slide deck)*
- **Panel discussions** with local regenerative leaders
- **Interactive workshops** using **Liberating Structures**
- **Open networking time** to build deeper relationships

### 4️⃣ Promote the Event

- Post on **Twitter, LinkedIn, Telegram, and Discord**
- Tag [@ReFiDAOist](https://twitter.com/ReFiDAOist) and [@ReFiEvents](https://twitter.com/refievents) for cross-promotion
- Engage in **community groups and relevant forums**

### 5️⃣ Capture & Share Content

- Take **high-quality photos/videos** during key moments
- Publish a **post-event recap** (Twitter thread, blog post, LinkedIn update)
- Share **analytics & feedback** to improve future events

💡 **Example Event Analytics:** [ReFi Lisboa Event Analytics 2023](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Fe0U75dzVV4j6cE_Zk8o37n3GPsqx_oAV_D-y0-q0Sk/edit?usp=sharing)
